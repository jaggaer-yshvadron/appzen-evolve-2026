08 · One Platform

Why run it all in JAGGAER?

An invoice touches sourcing, contracts, POs, receipts and payment. Every step that lives outside your suite is a step your data, your audit trail and your AI can't see.

STITCHED TOOLS
  • Invoice data exported, transformed, re-imported: copies go stale
  • Two audit trails, two permission models, two logins for suppliers
  • AI sees a fragment: an answer that can't reach the PO or the contract
  • Integration tax on every upgrade, every new field, every flow change
ONE JAGGAER SUITE
  • One data model source-to-pay: JAI cites the whole chain, not a fragment
  • One audit trail from requisition to payment, one set of permissions
  • Suppliers onboarded once: one portal, one status view, one payment track
  • Deep Research across everything: spend, suppliers, invoices, pay in one analysis
Same invoice. Same supplier. Same data. That's the gain.
